Guidance for Analysis

For each selected term, address the following:
	1.	Whether the term is a continuant or an occurrent
	2.	Its appropriate BFO category
	3.	What entity bears the role, function, or disposition (if applicable)
	4.	At least one relevant relation (e.g., participates in, bearer of, part of)
	5.	A brief justification for the classification using BFO principles
